DATA AND FIGURES

All quantities are dimensionless. The threshold is (b/c)*_n(alpha).
L is a model parameter but is omitted from the threshold's notation.

Columns
L          Number of states in each periodic phenotype coordinate.
n          Number of phenotype coordinates.
alpha      Strength of discrimination by phenotypic distance.
mu         Scaled strategy mutation rate, Nu/2.
nu         Scaled phenotype mutation rate, Nv/2.
threshold  Critical benefit-to-cost ratio for stationary cooperation
           abundance under weak selection in the large-population limit.
distance   Periodic distance in Figure 1.
helping_probability   exp(-alpha * distance).

Files in reference_results/data/ (and results/data/ after a new run)
fig1_kernel.csv: 15 rows. L=5, n=2, alpha=0.3,1,3; distance=0,...,4.
  The schematic uses phenotypes (1,1) and (3,4), at periodic distance 4.
fig2.csv: 600 rows. n=2, mu=0.5, nu=1; L=2,3,4,5,100;
  120 linearly spaced alpha values from 0.1 to 5.
fig3.csv: 50 rows. alpha=1, mu=0.5, nu=1; L=2,3,4,5,100; n=1,...,10.
fig4.csv: 10,000 rows. L=5, n=2, mu=0.5;
  100 linear alpha values from 0.1 to 4 and 100 logarithmic nu values
  from 10^(-1) to 10^(2.5).
fig5.csv: 14,400 rows. L=5, n=2, nu=3;
  120 linear alpha values from 0.1 to 4 and 120 logarithmic mu values
  from 10^(-2) to 10^(1.5).
fig5_threshold_vs_n.csv: 30 rows. L=5, mu=0.5, nu=1;
  alpha=0.3,0.5,4; n=1,...,10.
fig7_threshold_vs_L.csv: 72 rows. n=2, mu=0.5, nu=1;
  alpha=0.5,1.5,3; L=2,...,25.

The six threshold tables have columns L,n,alpha,mu,nu,threshold.
fig1_kernel.csv has columns L,n,alpha,distance,helping_probability.

The same heat-map grids are also supplied as compressed NumPy files:
fig4_values.npz: alpha[100], nu[100], threshold[100,100].
  threshold[i,j] corresponds to nu[i] and alpha[j].
fig5_values.npz: alpha[120], mu[120], threshold[120,120].
  threshold[i,j] corresponds to mu[i] and alpha[j].
In these CSV tables, alpha varies fastest within each nu or mu value.

The saved thresholds are not clipped. Heat-map colors saturate at 10;
contour lines use the raw values. Figure 2 displays thresholds from
2 to 10, and fig7_threshold_vs_L uses a logarithmic vertical axis.
Horizontal dotted lines at 1 (fig2 and fig5_threshold_vs_n) or 3
(fig3) are visual reference lines.
